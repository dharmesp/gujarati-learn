# How to publish Gujarati Match & Learn

You publish from the computer once. After that the child only needs the phone.

## First time (about 10 minutes)

1. Go to **https://dash.cloudflare.com/sign-up** and make a free account.
2. In the left menu open **Workers & Pages** → **Create** → **Pages** → **Upload assets**
   (it may say "Drag and drop your files").
3. Project name: `gujarati-learn` (any name you like). This becomes your link.
4. Drag the whole **gujarati-match-game** folder onto the upload box → **Deploy**.
5. Your permanent link appears, like **https://gujarati-learn.pages.dev**.

## Put it on the child's phone

1. Open the link on the phone **with internet on**.
2. Install it:
   - **iPhone / iPad:** use **Safari** → Share button (square with arrow) → **Add to Home Screen** → Add.
   - **Android:** use **Chrome** → ⋮ menu → **Install app** (or **Add to Home screen**).
3. From now on, open the game **from the new icon** ("Gujarati Learn").
   Progress is saved inside that app on that phone.

## Updating the game later

1. Change the files on the computer.
2. Cloudflare → your project → **Create new deployment** → drag the folder again → Deploy.
3. On the phone, open the app once with internet. The new version appears
   the **next** time the app is opened. Progress is kept.

## Good to know

- Works offline after it has been opened once with internet.
- Progress lives on the phone. A different phone or browser starts fresh.
- On iPhone, the Home Screen app and Safari keep **separate** progress. Always use the icon.
- Parents area: home screen → **⚙️ For parents** (answer a small sum). Reset, clear data,
  or choose which letter to start from.
